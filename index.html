About FysiSteps

Empowering real, tangible environmental restoration through computer vision verification and community accountability

🌿🌍

Our Mission

FysiSteps was built on a simple conviction: the environmental crisis will not be solved by passive pledges or digital greenwashing. It requires real, physical steps on the ground—cleaning garbage dumps, planting living flora, conserving natural waterways, and repairing our common spaces.

Every activity uploaded to FysiSteps goes through our rigorous deep inspection engine. With mandatory video proof, authentic before-and-after photo verification, and a 48-hour cadence between uploads, FysiSteps guarantees that every single coin awarded and tree recorded represents physical, unalterable proof of ecological restoration.

🔬

AI & DeepFake Defense

Our scanning pipeline inspects digital artifacts, lighting consistencies, and image duplication. Synthetic generative AI images and recycled photos are strictly rejected.

⏱️

48-Hour Cadence

Meaningful environmental work takes time and dedication. To prevent spam and prioritize genuine quality impact, warriors are eligible to submit one verified deed every 48 hours.
